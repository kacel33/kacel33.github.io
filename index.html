Research

My research interests include efficient neural networks and vision-language models (VLMs). I have previously worked on binary neural networks, exploring extreme quantization for model compression. Currently, I focus on improving the robustness and reliability of VLMs, particularly in the areas of negation understanding, debiasing, and test-time adaptation.
